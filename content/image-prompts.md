## Style block (paste before every prompt)

Photorealistic, candid documentary-style photograph for a family-owned financial services firm in New Jersey. Real middle-class families and advisors, natural and unposed, genuine expressions, nobody looking stiff or staged. Warm natural light (morning or late afternoon), soft shadows, true-to-life skin tones. Settings are real lived-in homes and bright, modern but modest suburban offices, never glass skyscrapers or luxury interiors. Wardrobe is everyday and relaxed; advisors wear navy, white or light blue. Color grading slightly warm with muted blues in the background. Shot on a 35mm or 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the people. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no visible brand names, no AI artifacts in hands or faces.
