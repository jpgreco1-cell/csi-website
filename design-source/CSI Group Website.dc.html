06 · ONE FAMILY, THREE SERVICES

What it looks like when one team handles it all.

One household, one lead advisor, three services in the first year. Here is how that usually unfolds.

Year one · Taxes

It started with a tax return.

Marco and Elena Ramirez, both in their early 50s with two kids in college, came to us for one reason: their return. Their CPA prepared it and, while reading it, noticed an old 401(k) from a previous job and a beneficiary form that still named Marco’s mother. She did not pitch anything. She put a note in their file for the rest of the team.

Month three · Retirement

A checkup put everything on one table.

The CSI360™ Checkup looked at all seven pillars together. Their financial advisor mapped out when each of them could retire, which accounts to draw from first, and when to claim Social Security, using the real tax picture the CPA already had.

Month six · Wills and estate

The paperwork finally agreed.

Their in-house attorney updated both wills, named guardians and backup beneficiaries, and corrected the old forms, so every account, policy and document now says the same thing.

What changed for them

  • One call and one file, so they stopped explaining themselves four times.
  • Their tax return, retirement plan and wills now agree with each other.
  • Every year the same team picks up where they left off, and adds what the family needs next.

OUR HISTORY

It started on a side street in Brooklyn.

CSI Group’s story began in 1973, when Peter Greco opened a small tax practice on a side street in Brooklyn, New York. For the immigrant community he served, navigating taxes, insurance, and financial paperwork was often overwhelming. While affluent communities had access to these services, middle-class families were largely left on their own. Peter changed that, becoming a trusted resource and advocate for hardworking families who needed clarity and support.

By 1983, word of Peter’s value to the community had spread, leading him to open a state-of-the-art office on 86th Street in Bensonhurst. It quickly became the go-to destination for financial help in the neighborhood. Throughout the 1980s, he expanded the firm’s team and services, always guided by the evolving needs and worries of his clients.

In 1990, CSI expanded to New Jersey, opening its first location in Matawan. The firm’s focus shifted from serving primarily immigrant families to meeting the needs of suburban, middle-class households. Still, the mission remained unchanged: to make high-quality tax, financial, insurance, and estate planning services accessible to everyday families, not just the wealthy.

A new generation

When Peter passed away, the next generation of leadership, his sons James and Robert Greco, took the helm as co-CEOs. They set out to honor their father’s legacy while building the firm of the future.

Under their leadership, CSI has transformed into a next-generation financial services firm. Through CSI360™, clients receive fully integrated tax, investment, retirement, insurance, and estate planning services, coordinated by a single advisor who manages the big picture. This advisor-led model simplifies complexity, provides clarity, and builds lasting trust through every stage of life.

Where we are today

Today, CSI Group serves more than 22,000 households across 20 branches in New Jersey and New York, and is expanding with an ambitious vision to serve every Main Street in America. With a team of more than 80 professionals and growing, CSI is scaling with purpose, investing in talent, and aligning its culture around one shared mission: to elevate what it means to be a client-first financial firm.

At CSI, every client is still treated like family, just as Peter Greco intended when he first opened his doors more than 50 years ago.

"Financial services have long been fragmented and reserved for the ultra-wealthy. We are changing that by bringing everything under one roof and delivering it with the high-touch experience our clients deserve."

James Greco, Co-CEO
